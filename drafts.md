# Monday review — drafts (original, written in-session)

Draft A:
Monday arrived like a software update nobody asked for: slower, buggier, and it restarted my whole week.

Draft B:
I gave my Monday one star: the coffee was cold, the 9am meeting could have been an email, and the week hadn't even started lying yet.

Draft C:
My Monday's highlight was the moment I realised the "quick sync" was a calendar invite for my own funeral, scheduled by my boss.

Draft D:
Monday reviewed: alarm snoozed itself, inbox tripled by 9am, and even my coffee filed a complaint about the workload.

Draft E:
Monday: woke up tired, worked tired, went to bed tired — the only thing that showed up on time was the Monday itself.

## Revision — pick B and tighten it

B is the strongest: review framing ("one star"), three concrete Monday details,
and the turn lands at the end. Trimmed for speed (criterion 4: no wasted words):

Final candidate:
I gave my Monday one star: cold coffee, a 9am meeting that could've been an email, and a week that hadn't even started lying yet.

Check: still a review of MY Monday, concrete (coffee, 9am meeting), deadpan,
repeatable to a coworker. "could've been an email" is the relatable laugh;
"hadn't even started lying yet" is the turn.

Notes:
- A is a clean tech metaphor, lands fast, but "software update" is a bit common.
- B has three concrete details (cold coffee, 9am meeting, week lying) — good structure, maybe long.
- C is darker/absurd; "funeral" might read as a joke about something else, risky vs criterion 1.
- D is punchy and concrete; "coffee filed a complaint" is the turn.
- E is relatable but mostly generic tiredness (criterion 3 weakness).
